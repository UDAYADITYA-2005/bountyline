# Sample photos

Put the JPEG files named in `../scenarios.yaml` here, and log each one in `../CREDITS.md`.
The ingest step re-encodes them, so any size from 1,000 to 4,000 px is fine.

Where to look:

- USFWS National Digital Library and NOAA Fisheries photo galleries (search "northern pikeminnow",
  "northern pike", "smallmouth bass").
- Wikimedia Commons, filtering for public domain or CC licences.
- iNaturalist observations with CC0 or CC BY licences.

Your own photos are better than any of these: see `../../programs/own-photos.template.yaml`.
