# Sample photo credits

Every file in `samples/photos/` needs a row before it is committed. Prefer, in order: works of the
US federal government (US Fish and Wildlife Service, NOAA Fisheries, National Park Service), which
are usually public domain; CC0; CC BY or CC BY-SA with attribution. Check each photo's own licence
on its page; don't assume. No photos from stock sites, social media or news articles.

What makes a good sample photo: one whole fish, side view, in focus, plain background, good light,
at least 1,000 px on the long side. Photos with a ruler are better. If you only find a few
pikeminnow photos, crop different fish from the same source, but never reuse the same fish across
two "clean" scenarios (the duplicate check will catch it, correctly).

If you take your own photos, list them here too, with "own photo" as the source and your name as
the author.
